*The Dark Forest* is the first large vectarine **strategy** game. It has more polished graphics and shaders, using procedural generation
for rendering the planet textures.

It is replayable, with runs lasting about an hour. It has everything you'd think in a large 4X strategy game:
A large map, enemies, resources, technologies, etc.

For the best experience (fullscreen), you should play it on [itch.io](https://vanyle0.itch.io/the-dark-forest)

**Description**

Humanity's time is counted, resources are limited and diminishing quickly. We needs to expand to the stars. But nobody knows what we might find there.

What are these strange radio signals we some coming from the void?

The Dark Forest is a real-time strategy game about developing your civilisation, colonizing planets, building factories and staying quiet. It borrows elements from Stellaris, Polytopia and other 4X games, with a focus on stealth (I got the idea from books like Remembrance of Earth's Past series as well as Ring from Stephen Baxter).

To beat the game, your civilisation needs to build the ring, a structure able to open a gateway between dimensions to escape the dying universe.

You can expect your runs to take about 1 hours to beat the end-game.
