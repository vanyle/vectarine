**Description**

You were investigating rumours of undiscovered ruins of an ancient civilisation that caused the bronze age collapse. However, you fell in a sinkhole and cannot climb back up.
You will need to explore a gigantic cave system named the "Explorium" to find a way back to the surface.
Explorium features a mix of exploration, combat, story/lore, and puzzle solving. It has about 3 to 4 hours of gameplay.
